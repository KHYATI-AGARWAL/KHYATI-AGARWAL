When reporting inferential statistics, include the test used, test statistic, p-value,
and an effect size with confidence interval where possible. Also mention assumption checks
such as normality and homogeneity of variances.

For group comparisons, box plots are preferred over bar charts when distributions are skewed
or when sample sizes are small, because they show spread and potential outliers.

A p-value indicates compatibility with the null model, but practical importance should be
interpreted with effect size magnitude and confidence intervals.
