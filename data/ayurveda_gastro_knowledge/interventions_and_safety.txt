Common classical formulations discussed in gastro contexts include combinations involving ginger, pippali,
trikatu, musta, bilva, or avipattikara depending on pattern and tolerance. Panchakarma-oriented strategies
such as basti or virechana are typically considered after assessment of strength, season, and contraindications.

For research writing, report interventions as multi-component care bundles: dietary protocol, behavioral practices,
and herbal regimen with dose and duration. Include explicit safety language: screen for alarm symptoms such as GI
bleeding, persistent vomiting, progressive weight loss, severe anemia, dysphagia, fever, and suspected obstruction;
these require urgent biomedical evaluation and are outside self-management scope.
