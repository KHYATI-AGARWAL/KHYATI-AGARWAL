For functional dyspepsia and irritable bowel presentations, Ayurvedic assessment maps symptom clusters
into vata, pitta, or kapha dominance with attention to stress, sleep, and diet triggers. Vata-dominant
patterns may show pain, bloating, irregularity, and anxiety. Pitta-dominant patterns may show burning,
acid regurgitation, irritability, and loose motions. Kapha-dominant patterns may show nausea, heaviness,
slow digestion, and excessive salivation.

Dietary plans are individualized but typically include warm, freshly prepared foods, reduction of ultra-
processed meals, and graduated reintroduction after symptom stabilization. Lifestyle modules frequently
include breathing practices, circadian meal routines, and stress regulation, because gut-brain factors are
considered central in recurrent symptoms.
